Experimental feature — results may have ±1–3 mm error. Do not use as the sole basis for repair decisions.

You will load 2 to 4 photos of the same damage, taken from slightly different positions. The app will estimate the maximum depth or height of the damage.

How to take the photos:
• Use marker ID 1 (50 mm) or ID 2 (100 mm).
• Keep the marker and damage in the central 70% of the frame.
• Between each photo, move 3–6 cm sideways.
• Do not rotate the phone or change the distance to the surface.
• Same zoom (1×, main camera) for all photos.

Depth (inward dent) will show as negative. Height (outward bulge) will show as positive.

How to use

1 · Place the marker

Print one of the three ArUco markers and place it flat against the surface, next to the damage.

  • ID 0 (15 mm) — very small damage only. Less accurate; avoid if ID 1 fits.
  • ID 1 (50 mm) — primary marker for most inspections.
  • ID 2 (100 mm) — large damage where ID 1 looks too small.

2 · Take the photo

  • Use the phone's main camera at 1×. Never wide-angle (0.5×).
  • Keep the marker and the damage inside the central 70% of the frame.
  • Hold the phone as parallel to the surface as possible. A tilt warning fires when the marker looks noticeably deformed.
  • Good, even lighting. Avoid glare directly on the marker.

3 · Measure

  • The scale is set automatically when a marker is detected.
  • Tap 🎯 Auto-detect dim for assisted detection: tap once on the damage and the app proposes the two endpoints automatically. Drag to adjust if needed, then confirm.
  • Tap ➕ Add manual dim to place endpoints fully by hand. Use this when auto-detect fails or the damage has no clear edges.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom in for precise placement.
  • Tap the scale badge (bottom-left) to edit the reference value.

4 · Save and share

  • 💾 Save — downloads a JPEG with all dimensions drawn.
  • 📤 Share — sends the annotated image via the system share sheet.
  • ⊙ View original — hold to compare the rectified photo with the original.

5 · Depth estimate (experimental)

  • Tap 🧪 Depth / Height estimate in the Tools panel.
  • Load 2 to 4 photos of the same damage taken from slightly different positions (move 3–6 cm sideways between shots, same zoom and distance).
  • Results have ~15–25% error on non-reflective surfaces. Use as a guide, not as a precise measurement.

🎯 Auto-detect tips

  • Tap the centre of the damage, not the edge. The algorithm searches outward from that point.
  • Works best with a single clear object on a uniform background (dent on a flat panel, coin on a surface).
  • If the proposal is wrong, drag the endpoints or cancel and use ➕ Add manual dim instead.
  • Auto-detect only works when an ArUco marker was detected (not with manual calibration).

⚠ Accuracy limits

  • Error ~0.1% with calibrated device at image centre, ~0.5% uncalibrated, up to ~3% near the edges.
  • Marker ID 0 is unreliable (3–5% error). Use only when strictly necessary.
  • The tool measures surface extent only. The 🧪 depth estimate is experimental (±15–25% error).
